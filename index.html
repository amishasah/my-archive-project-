Navratangarh · Gumla
Palace & Fort

Navratangarh

Once a capital of the Nagvanshi dynasty, this fortified complex preserves the remains of a palace, water gate, moat, court and underground spaces. Its surviving masonry now stands as a fragment of a much larger royal settlement.

Palamu Fort · Latehar
Fort Ruins

Palamu Fort

Two medieval fortifications associated with the Chero rulers overlook the North Koel landscape near Betla. Weather, vegetation and time have reduced the once-defensive architecture to evocative sandstone ruins.

Kunda Fort · Chatra
Palace Ruins

Kunda Fort

The remains of the old Kunda palace survive near the present Kunda area. Government forest records describe standing walls in a considerably dilapidated condition, surrounded by the landscape that has reclaimed the site.

Ichak · Hazaribagh
Temple Town

Old Ichak

Ichak was once a capital of the Ramgarh Raj and developed a dense landscape of temples, ponds, gardens and palace remains. Many historic structures have suffered from neglect, leaving an unusually layered townscape of memory and decay.

Ruam · East Singhbhum
Fort Site

Old Fort at Ruam

Ruam is recorded by the Archaeological Survey of India as the site of an old fort. Little of the original defensive landscape remains legible today, making the site an example of how historical places can survive mainly through records.

Source

Benisagar · West Singhbhum
Archaeological Remains

Benisagar

Around the ancient tank at Benisagar are remains of temples, sculptures and structures recorded as a protected archaeological site. The surviving fragments suggest a once-active sacred and settlement landscape now dispersed around the water body.

Source

Arzimukhimpur · Sahibganj
Ruined Buildings

Baradari Ruins

The Archaeological Survey of India records ruined baradari buildings here, with probable underground cells and passages. Their incomplete remains preserve a rare architectural clue to an older layer of the region's built history.

Source
Archaeological Survey of India

Haradih · Ranchi
Temple Remains

Temples at Haradih

Haradih contains an archaeological group of temple remains that is listed among Jharkhand's centrally protected sites. What survives offers a small, weathered window into the region's older religious architecture.
